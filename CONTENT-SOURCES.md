## Deliberately missing

- Original CAD and version-specific renders.
- Verified test setup, convergence, uncertainty and matched-condition reports.
- Personal biographies and individual contributions.
- Dated manufacturing/testing milestones and corresponding photographs.

These are content gaps, represented with explicit editable placeholders. The site does not fabricate records to fill them.
